How Photosynthesis Powers Life on Earth

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants, algae and some bacteria convert light energy into chemical energy.

Inside the chloroplasts, the pigment chlorophyll absorbs sunlight. This energy is used to split water molecules and release oxygen.

The plant then combines carbon dioxide from the air with hydrogen to build glucose, a sugar that stores energy.

For example, a single large tree can release enough oxygen in a season to support several people. Because nearly every food chain begins with plants, photosynthesis is the foundation of almost all life on Earth.
